Who you are buying from

A small Australian business, which is the point.

The large travel eSIM brands are support desks in another time zone reselling the same underlying networks we do. What is different here is who picks up when it goes wrong, and which country's consumer law you are covered by.

We are not going to pretend to be bigger than we are. There is one person answering messages today. That is a limit and it is also the reason it gets answered.

Australian consumer guarantees

You are covered by Australian Consumer Law, not by terms written in a jurisdiction you have never been to.

A refund if it never worked

If the eSIM never activated on your trip, you get your money back. We do not ask you to prove it with screenshots.

We say so when it went wrong

Outages and network problems get written up on this site rather than quietly closed as a support ticket.
