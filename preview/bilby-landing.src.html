How it works

Three steps, and none of them happen at the airport.

The thing that goes wrong in this category is a traveller standing at arrivals at eleven at night trying to install something. So Bilby moves all of it earlier.

1

Pick where you are going

Choose a country and a size. The full price is on the card before you pay, including what happens if the eSIM never activates.

2

Install it at home

One scan on your own couch, on your own wifi, with time to spare. Nothing switches on yet and nothing starts counting down.

3

Land already connected

Turn the phone on and it finds a local network by itself. No app to open and no code to type while you are carrying a bag and a passport.

Why Bilby

A small Australian business, which is the point.

The large travel eSIM brands are support desks in another time zone reselling the same underlying networks. What differs is who picks up when it goes wrong.

Someone answers

A person in Australian hours who can see your profile and fix it. Not a form, and not a bot that asks you to restart your phone.

Australian consumer law

You are covered by Australian Consumer Law, not by terms written in a jurisdiction you have never been to.

A refund if it never worked

If the eSIM never activated on your trip you get your money back, and we do not ask you to prove it with screenshots.

Nothing renews behind you

The plan ends when the trip ends. There is no subscription quietly billing you three months after you got home.
