How it works

Three quiet steps, and none of them happen at the airport.

The best time to sort out your phone is on your own sofa, with wifi, a week before you go.

Before you fly

We check your handset can take an eSIM, then you add the profile once. A minute at home, on wifi, where nothing is urgent.

When you land

It attaches by itself. Nothing to open, nothing to type, no code to find while the seatbelt sign is still on.

If something is off

Message us on WhatsApp from the airport wifi and a person replies. That is the one channel that still works when your data does not.
